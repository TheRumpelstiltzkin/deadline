FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What Canadian deadlines does DeadlineHQ track?
CRA deadlines including T2 corporate returns, T1 self-employment returns, GST/HST quarterly and annual remittances, payroll source deductions (CPP, EI, income tax), T4/T4A/T5 information returns, and instalment payments. Provincial deadlines including PST (BC, SK, MB), QST (Québec), workers' compensation (WSIB, WorkSafeBC, WCB), business licence renewals, health and fire inspections, annual returns, and industry-specific permits. If it has a due date and a Canadian penalty, we track it.
I already have an accountant. Do I still need this?
Yes! Your accountant handles T2 and GST/HST filings, but what about your municipal business licence renewal? Your health inspection? Your commercial insurance? Your WSIB premium reconciliation? Most deadlines fall through the cracks between you and your accountant. DeadlineHQ keeps both of you aligned — you can even share your dashboard with them directly.
How do you know what deadlines apply to my business?
When you sign up, you select your business type (corporation, sole proprietorship, partnership, etc.), your province or territory, and your industry. We use this to pre-load a template of all common CRA, provincial, and municipal deadlines. You can then add, remove, or customize any deadline to match your exact situation. For example, an Ontario restaurant gets WSIB, health inspection, liquor licence, and PST-exempt deadlines automatically.
Does this work for Québec businesses with Revenu Québec?
Absolutely. Québec businesses have additional provincial filing requirements including QST remittances, Relevé slips (RL-1, RL-3), CNESST premiums, and provincial corporate tax returns. Our Québec templates include both CRA and Revenu Québec deadlines with filing instructions for each agency. The Business plan includes direct links to both CRA My Business Account and Mon dossier pour les entreprises.
What happens if I miss a deadline anyway?
Our escalation system makes that very unlikely — you'll get reminders at 30, 14, 7, 3, and 1 day before each deadline via email and SMS. But if you do miss one, each deadline card includes CRA penalty calculations, interest rates, late-filing instructions, and information about the CRA Voluntary Disclosures Program (VDP) to help you minimize the damage.
Is my data stored in Canada?
Yes. All data is stored on Canadian servers (AWS Canada — Montreal region) in compliance with PIPEDA (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act) and applicable provincial privacy legislation. We never share your data with third parties.
Can I cancel anytime?
Absolutely. No contracts, no commitments. Cancel from your dashboard in two clicks. If you're on an annual plan, we'll refund the prorated unused portion. Your data stays accessible for 30 days after cancellation in case you change your mind.
